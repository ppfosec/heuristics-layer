INTERVIEWER: A vendor answers a technical access-control question by saying it has a SOC 2 report. What do you notice?

EXPERT: A report only helps if the service and control are actually in scope. I am not dismissing the report. I am refusing to let it answer a larger question than the auditor examined.

INTERVIEWER: When could the report be enough?

EXPERT: If the exact service, control, and applicable period are covered, and there is no relevant exception, it may be the best evidence I need for that bounded question. The novice mistake is turning “SOC 2” into a general security verdict.
