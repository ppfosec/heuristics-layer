INTERVIEWER: You confirmed a control gap. Do you escalate it?

EXPERT: A real gap does not automatically deserve escalation. I ask what can plausibly happen, what is exposed, how dependent we are, and whether the effect is reversible.

INTERVIEWER: Would the same gap ever produce opposite decisions?

EXPERT: Of course. Missing separation in a disposable test system is not the same as the identical gap over regulated production data. The control statement is the same. The consequence is not.

INTERVIEWER: Is there always a numeric threshold?

EXPERT: No. Sometimes policy gives one. Often it is a qualitative accountable decision, and pretending I have a precise number would make the record worse.
