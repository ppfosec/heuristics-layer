# What Heuristics Layer is trying to capture

General-purpose models already know a great deal about controls, frameworks, certifications, contracts, and common security practices. Heuristics Layer focuses on the experienced work between those facts and a defensible decision.

## Decision lenses

Use these lenses to notice what deserves another interview question. They are not a checklist and not every lens belongs in every case.

### Material consequence

- What business process, customer promise, regulated activity, or critical dependency is exposed?
- Which failure modes matter enough to alter the decision?
- Is the gap technically real but operationally immaterial?

### Evidence and uncertainty

- Is the source an assertion, design description, operating evidence, or independent test?
- What does the evidence actually cover?
- Which uncertainty remains after reviewing it?
- Is the uncertainty itself material enough to delay or escalate?

### Incentives and power

- Who benefits from each option?
- Who pays, implements, supports, or inherits the risk?
- Who can formally approve, informally block, or quietly bypass the decision?
- Which stated objection may be standing in for cost, status, workload, or loss of autonomy?

### Adoption and operating reality

- Will the team use the selected process or tool?
- Has this team bypassed imposed controls before?
- Does familiarity, identity, or vendor affinity materially affect adoption?
- Would a stronger formal control produce a weaker real control because people route around it?

### Relationships and communication

- What history exists between the practitioner and the stakeholder?
- Which framing will preserve agency and produce useful disclosure?
- When should the practitioner advise, negotiate, document, escalate, or stop pushing?
- How should the same trade-off be explained to security, finance, engineering, procurement, or an executive?

### Constraints and commitments

- Which requirements are contractual, regulatory, architectural, or otherwise non-negotiable?
- Which controls can be compensated for, and which cannot?
- What deadline, budget, customer commitment, or technical dependency narrows the options?

### Reversibility and sequencing

- Can the decision be piloted, limited, monitored, or reversed?
- Which question or experiment creates the most useful information next?
- Is a temporary decision acceptable while evidence improves?

### Bias and alternative explanations

- Is the expert treating organizational history as destiny?
- Is brand preference being mistaken for evidence?
- Does a familiar team receive more trust than its artifacts justify?
- What plausible interpretation would lead another experienced practitioner to act differently?

## The desired knowledge object

A useful heuristic is a bounded, evidence-linked pattern connecting a situation to a better question, intervention, explanation, escalation, or decision. It should preserve the human and organizational conditions that make the pattern work.

Domain knowledge may appear in the artifact as context. It is not the principal extraction target.
