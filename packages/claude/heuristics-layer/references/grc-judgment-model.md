# GRC judgment model

Look beyond control facts and examine the machinery connecting evidence to action.

- **Material consequence:** business process, customer promise, regulated activity, dependency, and plausible failure.
- **Evidence and uncertainty:** assertion versus operating evidence, scope, residual uncertainty, and whether uncertainty itself is material.
- **Incentives and power:** who benefits, pays, implements, approves, blocks, bypasses, or inherits risk.
- **Adoption:** whether the stronger formal option will be used, and whether people will route around it.
- **Relationships and communication:** prior trust, useful framing, preserved agency, and when to advise, negotiate, document, or escalate.
- **Constraints:** contractual, regulatory, architectural, budget, deadline, and customer commitments.
- **Reversibility and sequence:** pilots, limits, monitoring, temporary decisions, and the next question that buys information.
- **Bias:** familiarity, brand preference, hindsight, uneven trust, stereotypes, and alternative explanations.

A useful heuristic is a bounded, evidence-linked pattern that improves a future question, intervention, explanation, escalation, or decision. Preserve the human and organizational conditions that make it work.
