# Interview modes and briefings

The framework supports five initial modes:

- `deep_dive`: stay with one judgment area until its cues, thresholds, exceptions, and consequences are explicit.
- `case_review`: follow a real or sanitized case in decision order.
- `contrast`: compare good and bad, material and immaterial, or sufficient and insufficient cases.
- `failure_analysis`: examine when the expert's first judgment was wrong.
- `novice_comparison`: identify what a competent junior would miss or ask in the wrong order.

`createInterviewBriefing` retrieves existing coverage for a topic and combines it with unresolved questions, contradictions, candidate patterns, and probes from prior notes. The briefing tells the interviewer what is already known, what should not be duplicated, and which thread deserves another pass.

Keep briefings short. Their job is to make the next conversation sharper, not to turn a voice interview into a deposition.
